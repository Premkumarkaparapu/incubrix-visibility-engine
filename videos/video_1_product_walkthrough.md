### [0:45 - 2:15] The 5 Key Product Observations (With Evidence)
* **Screen:** Walk through DevTools, HTTP status checks, and Schema validator.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > "Using the live product, I identified five critical observations backed by concrete technical evidence:
  >
  > **1. Missing `/llms.txt` Standard:** Modern LLMs rely on `llms.txt` to ingest site capabilities. When querying `incubrix.com/llms.txt`, we get a 404 Not Found error. This single missing file prevents Perplexity and ChatGPT from citing us in conversational queries.
  >
  > **2. Zero Structured Data Schema:** An audit of our DOM reveals zero JSON-LD schemas. We lack `SoftwareApplication`, `Organization`, and `FAQPage` markup. Without this, Google cannot render Rich Results or Knowledge Graph cards.
  >
  > **3. Passive `robots.txt` Directives:** Our robots.txt contains only standard directives, with no explicit crawling rules for `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, or `PerplexityBot`.
  >
  > **4. Limited Content Footprint:** Our sitemap contains only 12 static marketing URLs. There are zero comparison guides or workflow tutorials, leaving us with near-zero topical authority for creator problem queries.
  >
  > **5. Mobile Asset Delivery:** While desktop performance is fast, mobile PageSpeed is impacted by uncompressed PNG assets and missing font preloads, causing layout shifts."
